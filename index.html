Photo and the Illustrated styles are the only parts that use the network. Photo adds a random timestamp to each request so the browser never reuses a face; if the image fails to load, the illustrated avatar is shown instead. Faces from thispersondoesnotexist carry a small watermark in the bottom-right corner; crop it before uploading. The Illustrated styles are drawn in your browser by DiceBear, loaded from a CDN only when you pick one; only its CC0 styles are offered, so no credit is needed.
